Our Story

From a Living Room in Delhi to 5 Lac+ Smiles Across India

Celebration Events® was born when our founders attempted to book a simple anniversary decoration for their parents. What followed was three cancelled local vendors, broken promises, and peeling wall paint that took months to repaint.

We recognized that party decoration in India needed a modern revolution: guaranteed slot bookings, verified punctual technicians, clean wall-safe adhesives, and transparent digital payments. Today, Celebration Events® is honored to be present in over 100 cities with a network of 1,500+ master stylists.
